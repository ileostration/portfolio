04 Helm

An AI that moves money asks first, shows its work and lets you take it back.

Step 1 of 5

Helm proposes.You decide.

A proposal card offers three choices: Not now, Why?, or Move $400. Nothing moves until you tap.

Step 2 of 5

It shows its work.

The sheet lists the history behind the suggestion and the exact rule Helm used.

Step 3 of 5

You confirmevery transfer.

Face ID confirms the move before any money leaves Ballast.

Step 4 of 5

You have 10 minutesto change your mind.

The toast counts down in real time. Undo puts both balances back.

Step 5 of 5

Every action,in plain language.

The activity log records what happened, when, and why.

Helm permission

You choose how far Helm can go.

Try each setting. The demo shows what Helm may do on its own and what it must always ask.

10 Coda

I start with one calm idea: money should feel level. Every choice, from research to motion, is tested against it. Directing a product means carrying that idea intact from a proposal card to an undo toast.
