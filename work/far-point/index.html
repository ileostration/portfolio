Night one fails
in five ways.

Smart telescopes promise one tap to the stars. The category — including telescopes from ZWO, Vaonis, Unistellar and DWARFLAB — is judged almost entirely on that first hour outdoors. The first night fails in predictable ways, and one failure is dangerous: pointing at the sun without a filter.

04 20:04 · SUN −6° · VEGA

Six ideas that
changed the guide.

COGNITIVE LOAD · SWELLER

One action per step. Only the parts involved in that action are drawn.

MULTIMEDIA · MAYER

The active part sits in cyan. Labels sit on the drawing. Twelve steps stay short.

GULFS OF EXECUTION · NORMAN

The exact hand position and a visible confirmation on every single step.

MINIMAL MANUAL · CARROLL

Real tasks first. Errors are anticipated rather than footnoted.

POKA-YOKE

A keyed mount and a lens-cap slot make the wrong move impossible, not merely unwise.

ANSI Z535.6

One safety message: signal word, hazard, consequence, avoidance — every time.

3. INITIAL SETUP AND CALIBRATION

Before use, ensure the equatorial base is positioned on stable, level ground. Extend each leg of the tripod fully and confirm that the locking collars are engaged. Refer to Appendix C for latitude adjustment on the German equatorial variant.

Connect the mount controller to the primary optical tube assembly using the supplied cabling, taking care to align the keyway. Power should not be applied until Section 4 has been reviewed in full.

4.2 Never point the optical tube at the sun without a certified full-aperture solar filter correctly fitted. Failure to observe this warning may result in permanent eye or sensor damage.

Once calibration is complete, proceed to Section 5 for network configuration. Bluetooth pairing requires the companion application, available for supported mobile operating systems, version 8 and above.

Users experiencing difficulty during first-time setup should consult the troubleshooting matrix in Appendix F, or contact customer support during standard business hours.

Warranty coverage is void if the unit is disassembled by anyone other than an authorized service technician. See enclosed card for full terms.

One rule, printed
where you look.

WARNING
Hazard

Pointing the S1 at the sun without the solar filter.

Consequence

Instant, permanent sensor damage — and eye injury if anyone looks through the optics.

Avoidance

Fit the magnetic solar filter before any daytime use. The app refuses solar targets without it.
