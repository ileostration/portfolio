( Killed direction )

Render-first

  1. 01

    Slow. A full-bleed image sets the weight of the page before a single word loads.

  2. 02

    Generic. Swap the logo and it could be any of the twelve practices in the audit.

  3. 03

    Says nothing about judgment. It shows the beauty of the result and hides the thinking behind it.

( Wireframe evolution )

The same homepage, three times.

Drag the dimension line. Left of it the page is near-final, just right of it a greyscale wireframe, beyond that the first sketch.

  1. Sketch

    Order before looks. One idea, three projects, one way to make contact, and nothing else on the page.

  2. Wireframe

    Nine navigation items became four, because Hick's law says fewer choices speed the first decision.

  3. Near-final

    The render became a numbered work list, and the footer title block says who drew the page and who checked it.

( Five theories )

Five reasons the index works.

Choose a sheet tab. Each theory is drawn once, then applied to this site.

Information foraging

Numbered labels give strong scent.

An A-200 label tells a visitor more than the word Studio does, before they click.

Progressive disclosure

Plan, then section, then axonometric, then detail.

Each project opens the way an architect explains a building: coarse first, fine last.

The Swiss grid

A visible 12-column grid signals rigor.

The grid stays on the page at 4 percent, so every alignment can be checked by eye.

Gestalt closure

Line drawings let the eye complete the volume.

A few lines carry a building, so the page does not need a 5 MB render to make its point.

Hick's law

Four sections speed the first decision.

Work, Studio, Journal and Contact replace the usual nine, and the first click comes sooner.

( Brief builder )

Four steps, two exits, three states.

The contact page is a flow, drawn before it was built. Each step asks one thing, and two decision points route visitors the practice cannot serve well to an honest answer early.

  1. 01 Site

    Address or neighbourhood, lot size if known.

  2. Decision

    Outside the Chicago area? Yes: a polite redirect, we work in Chicago and nearby. No: continue.

  3. 02 Budget

    Budget range, how it is funded.

  4. Decision

    Budget under $150,000? Yes: a smaller-scope path, a shorter brief with the same reply time. No: continue.

  5. 03 Timeline

    Preferred start, any fixed deadline.

  6. 04 Program

    Building type, rooms or areas, anything else.

( Design system )

A kit of parts,drawn once.

( Approach )

One kit, reused on every sheet. Colour is five values, type is three faces, and every mark sits on a 24 px grid. Nothing here is a photograph or a render.

01

Colour

Click a slab to copy its hex. Each slab lists the pairs it is used in and their contrast ratios.

Rule #C9C6BE is for grid, hatch and dividers only, never for text. In Blueprint the rule is paper at 18 percent.

02

Type

Archivo for display, with a width axis from 62 to 125. Inter for reading. JetBrains Mono for labels, numbers and coordinates.
